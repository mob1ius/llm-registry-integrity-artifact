things -- raw search pool per anchor went from 60 to 200 (HF's API takes
limit=200 in one call, no pagination needed), and target repos per anchor

canonical_base_model_id wrong is exactly the kind of mistake that bit us
in v1, and doing that at the same time as a scale-up risks stacking two
changes together and not knowing which one broke something if it does.
